# WhatsApp personal reply minimum correctness repair

Observed 2026-10-05 IST. This changes the existing reply path only. The Baileys bridge, generator, style profiles, encrypted store, and historical owner approvals were preserved. Generated auto-reply remains disabled; no WhatsApp message was sent for this evaluation.

## Changes

The reply agent now classifies answerability and speaker direction before generation. Missing owner location, meal state, availability, future decisions, and meeting confirmation are held for owner input. Technical status without same-thread confirmation is held for verified tool evidence. Unresolved references are held for clarification. Simple acknowledgements require no reply. These rules cover common English and Tanglish constructions; they are conservative heuristics, not a general semantic model.

The quality gate now checks answer direction and response substance independently of model self-confidence. A style score cannot pass a semantic failure. Confirmed same-thread meeting times cannot be changed, and new numeric facts in answers are rejected. The validator still cannot establish every implicit fact or nuanced conversational meaning; owner review remains required for generated drafts.

Historical examples are retrieved only when the turn may benefit from them. The contact index permits zero results and no longer forces a minimum number of weak matches. The cosine floor is 0.21, chosen from a small manually inspected development comparison: two clearly relevant top hits were 0.238 and 0.583; five clearly unrelated hits were at most 0.206. This is a provisional floor, not a calibrated retrieval accuracy estimate. The existing contact style profile remains available without retrieved examples.

Holdout reviews now store the overall five-level rating separately from semantic, dyadic, language, emoji, and length judgments. Existing ratings and private review sheets were preserved. A newly generated revision receives its own review case ID, so a prior owner rating cannot silently transfer to a different draft.
